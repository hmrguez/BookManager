# Wovenleaf

Shared language for the reader's library and reading experience.

## Language

**Personal tag**:
A reader-defined label used to organize books. A personal tag does not necessarily describe a book's genre.
_Avoid_: Genre (when referring to arbitrary organizational labels)

**Quarterly reading identity**:
A playful title and visual theme representing a reader's quarter, suggested from genre evidence or chosen by the reader even when evidence supports a suggestion. A reader's choice is not a classification of individual books or evidence for a genre count.
_Avoid_: Verified most-read genre (for a reader-selected identity), permanent reader type

**Review badge**:
A collectible achievement recognizing supported reading activity during a quarter. A review may feature several badges, combining milestone development with personal-best recognition.
_Avoid_: Quarterly reading identity (for an activity achievement), overall reader rank

**Pathway reveal**:
The closing discovery of the quarter's reading identity after its achievement badges have been revealed.
_Avoid_: Reading plan, prescribed next reads

**Reading visit**:
A period of engagement with one work and activity type that may span brief interruptions. Its length counts supported active time, excluding pauses.
_Avoid_: Session event (for the reader's visit), elapsed app-open time
